How to play

Steering

Swipe anywhere on the board, tap the arrow pad, or use the arrow keys / WASD. You can keep swiping without lifting your finger. Pause with the pause button, Space, P or Esc.

The snake waits for your first direction at the start of each level, so take a moment to look around.

      Watch out for

        Stars

        Clear a level for one star. Clear it quickly for two or three: each level shows its target times before you start. Survival Run gives stars for score instead.

        Good to know

        You can move into the square your tail is leaving, because the tail moves out of the way first.
